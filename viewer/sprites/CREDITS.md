# Vehicle photo credits

The vehicle textures here were cut from photos on Wikimedia Commons, perspective-corrected to flat
side/front/rear views and scaled down. Derivatives of CC BY-SA photos are shared under the same licence.
Logos and liveries belong to their respective owners (Region of Waterloo / Grand River Transit, Metrolinx);
they are used here only to depict the vehicles on a non-commercial map.
