    Isometric UWaterloo

    A giant hand-built isometric map of the University of Waterloo main campus. Every building comes from OpenStreetMap, so hover or tap one to see what it is.

    Getting around

    Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Search by building code (DC, MC, E5) or name, then share the link: it opens straight to that building.

    What's moving?

    Cars, GRT and GO buses, the ION, students on the fields and the geese are a simulation, not live data. Buses follow their real routes and stop at real stops; the ION drops the crossing gates as it passes.

    Night on campus

    The map follows the real time in Waterloo (the clock at the top): the lights come on at dusk and go off at dawn. The moon button flips it until the next dusk or dawn. Night mode turns the lights on: street lamps along the roads, paths and lots, and windows lit floor by floor from each building's real height and levels. Dana Porter and DC stay busy late; the residences are home time.
